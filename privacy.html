Privacy Policy

Bot Watch by Pitchfork-and-Torch. Effective 15 August 2026.

Bot Watch is an unofficial iPhone and Apple Watch companion for glancing at Bot status and approvals. It is not an xAI product.

What we collect

This version can run in demo mode with no account. If you later sign into a live Bot service, the app stores only what it needs to work:

  • A short-lived access token in the Keychain on iPhone
  • Bot names, status, clipped summaries, and approval titles on the Watch
  • A last-known snapshot in the App Group so complications and the Watch still work offline

The Watch does not store full drafts, email bodies, refresh tokens, or vendor passwords.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash telemetry in this build
  • No tracking across apps or websites

Where data lives

Demo data is generated on device. Live sign-in, when enabled, talks to the Bot service you configure. We do not operate a separate analytics backend.

Your choices

Sign out from Settings to clear the session and Watch cache. Delete the app to remove local files and Keychain items for this app.
